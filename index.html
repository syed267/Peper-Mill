About Us

One brand for all your pulp and paper machinery needs. At PMPS, we focus on developing innovative, reliable, and technology-driven solutions to meet the evolving demands of the pulp and paper industry, with 45 years of expertise in pulp and paper machinery design, development, installation and servicing. We aim to deliver solutions that support efficient production, consistent quality, and long-term operational performance. Our experience spans projects across various production capacities, enabling us to cater to diverse industrial requirements. Right from complete paper-making machinery to specialized equipment and customized production solutions, we emphasize precision engineering, dependable performance, and practical design. We work to understand each customer's needs and provide suitable machinery solutions that align with their production goals.
